## How to Use This Storybook

<div className="tutorial-steps">

<div className="step">
  <div className="step-number">1</div>
  <div className="step-body">
    <h4>Browse stories in the sidebar</h4>
    <p>
      Use the left panel to expand categories (<strong>Three</strong>, <strong>C++</strong>, <strong>Html</strong>).
      Each folder contains a component with one or more story variants. Click any story name to open it.
    </p>
    <div className="tip">
      💡 Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> to open the search bar and jump to any story by name.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div className="step">
  <div className="step-number">2</div>
  <div className="step-body">
    <h4>Switch between Docs and Canvas</h4>
    <p>
      At the top of the preview area you'll see two tabs:
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Docs</strong> — rendered documentation with all story variants and their props table.</li>
      <li><strong>Canvas</strong> — isolated interactive preview of a single story variant.</li>
    </ul>
    <div className="tip">
      💡 Most stories open in Docs mode by default so you can read context before diving in.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div className="step">
  <div className="step-number">3</div>
  <div className="step-body">
    <h4>Interact with Controls</h4>
    <p>
      In <strong>Canvas</strong> mode, open the <strong>Controls</strong> panel at the bottom of the screen.
      Stories that expose Leva or args-based controls let you tweak parameters (colors, sizes,
      shader uniforms, speeds) in real time without touching code.
    </p>
    <div className="tip">
      💡 Not all stories have controls — if the panel is empty, the story is self-contained.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div className="step">
  <div className="step-number">4</div>
  <div className="step-body">
    <h4>Toggle the Theme</h4>
    <p>
      Click the <strong>paintbrush 🎨</strong> icon in the top toolbar to switch between
      <strong> Light</strong> and <strong> Dark</strong> themes. This propagates the
      <code>data-theme</code> attribute used by DaisyUI components and the custom
      3D background colors.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div className="step">
  <div className="step-number">5</div>
  <div className="step-body">
    <h4>Resize the viewport</h4>
    <p>
      Use the <strong>screen 📱</strong> icon in the toolbar to preview stories at common
      breakpoints — mobile, tablet, desktop — and custom sizes like Instagram Post (4:5)
      or Instagram Square (1:1).
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div className="step">
  <div className="step-number">6</div>
  <div className="step-body">
    <h4>Keyboard shortcuts</h4>
    <div className="shortcuts-grid">
      <div className="shortcut"><kbd>S</kbd><span>Toggle sidebar</span></div>
      <div className="shortcut"><kbd>A</kbd><span>Toggle addons panel</span></div>
      <div className="shortcut"><kbd>D</kbd><span>Toggle Docs/Canvas</span></div>
      <div className="shortcut"><kbd>F</kbd><span>Fullscreen</span></div>
      <div className="shortcut"><kbd>⌘K</kbd><span>Search stories</span></div>
      <div className="shortcut"><kbd>Alt+→</kbd><span>Next story</span></div>
      <div className="shortcut"><kbd>Alt+←</kbd><span>Previous story</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
